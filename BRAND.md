# Positivity Juice

**Pour good into your everyday.**

Positivity Juice is a wellness and optimism brand built around everyday rituals, reflection, and human connection. Explore free encouragement cards and Your Daily Sip, the brand’s free newsletter, at [Positivity Juice](https://positivityjuice.com/).

## Social voice

Write with warmth and grounded optimism. Start with a specific, recognizable moment and give readers room to find their own meaning. Use plain language and keep captions easy to read on a phone.

## Visual identity

Use the original blue and white Positivity Juice carton. The core palette is Juice Blue `#2BA6FA`, Deep Navy `#0F2E63`, Warm Cream `#FFF9ED`, and Pour Blue `#E9F2FE`. Use Sunshine `#FFD836` as an accent. Set short headings in Fredoka and body text in DM Sans.
